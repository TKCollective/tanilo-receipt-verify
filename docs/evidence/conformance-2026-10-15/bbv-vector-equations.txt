The key A is mixed-order and has order 8L. The point [L]A is not the identity and has order 8. So [k]A depends
on k mod 8 through A's torsion component, and the full k and k mod L can give different results.

Results

Reading: the signature satisfies the cofactored equation whichever way k is taken, and the cofactorless equation
only when k is reduced modulo L first, as in the RFC's illustrative verifier (RFC 8032 section 6). With k as RFC 8032 section 5.1.7 states it, the
full digest, the cofactorless equation does not hold. The vector's builder computes k mod L (build_vectors.py at
9d4cf2c, lines 232 and 241, reduce the digest mod L), which is why it reports both equations as holding.
